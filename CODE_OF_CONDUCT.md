# Community Data Libraries Code of Conduct

## Our Purpose

Community Data Libraries (CDL) is a free, open-source tool that helps people locate and explore data about their own communities. It depends on contributions from many kinds of people: residents, teachers, students, librarians, researchers, and software developers. This Code of Conduct sets out how we treat one another so that every one of them can take part with confidence.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and the CDL project team pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Who This Applies To

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone who takes part in CDL: the CDL project team, code contributors, people who suggest datasets, community library creators, librarians, and editors, teachers who use or give feedback on the Teacher View, and anyone who contacts the project.

In this document, the **CDL project team** means the team at the OPALS Lab who manage the project's repositories, data catalog, and community libraries.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, for example in code review or when a dataset suggestion is declined
* Welcoming newcomers, including people with no background in coding or data
* Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best for the overall community, not only for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Belittling someone because of their experience level, their role, or the community they come from
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
* Submitting datasets, library entries, or lesson materials that expose personal information about identifiable individuals, or that demean a community or group
* Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all CDL spaces:

* Repositories, issues, pull requests, and discussions in the [community-data-libraries](https://github.com/community-data-libraries) GitHub organization
* The CDL website and its community libraries, including dataset suggestions, library content, and contact and feedback forms
* Project email and other official project channels
* CDL meetings, workshops, and events, whether online or in person

It also applies when someone is officially representing CDL in public, for example by using a project email address, posting from a project account, or presenting CDL at an event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please tell us. You do not need proof, and you do not need to be the person directly affected.

* Email the CDL project team at [info@communitydatalibraries.org](mailto:info@communitydatalibraries.org).
* If your report concerns a member of the CDL project team, or you would prefer not to use that address, email the project's Principal Investigator at [klboles@utk.edu](mailto:klboles@utk.edu).
* You may report on someone else's behalf, and you may ask that your name be withheld from the person the report concerns.

It helps to include what happened, when and where it happened, and any links or screenshots.

**What happens next.** We will acknowledge your report within five to seven business days, review it promptly and fairly, and tell you the outcome once the review is complete. Anyone with a conflict of interest in a report will take no part in reviewing it.

**Confidentiality.** The CDL project team will protect the privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident. Details are shared only with the people needed to resolve it.

**No retaliation.** Retaliating against someone for making a report in good faith, or for taking part in a review, is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct and will be treated as one.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The CDL project team is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior it finds inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The CDL project team has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, dataset suggestions, community library content, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Enforcement Guidelines

The CDL project team will follow these guidelines in deciding the consequences of a violation.

### 1. Correction

**Impact**: Inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning that explains the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Impact**: A violation through a single incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior, and no interaction with the people involved, including those enforcing this Code of Conduct, for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

**Impact**: A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: Removal from CDL spaces, including contributor or editor access, for a specified period.

### 4. Permanent Removal

**Impact**: A pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: Permanent removal from CDL spaces.

## Relationship to Other Policies

This Code of Conduct does not replace the policies of a participant's own institution or the terms of the platforms the project uses. Participants affiliated with a university or other organization remain subject to that organization's policies, and some reports may need to be referred to the appropriate office.

## Review

The CDL project team will review this Code of Conduct at least once a year, and whenever the project adds a new kind of community space. Suggestions are welcome through a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/community-data-libraries/web/issues) or at [info@communitydatalibraries.org](mailto:info@communitydatalibraries.org).
