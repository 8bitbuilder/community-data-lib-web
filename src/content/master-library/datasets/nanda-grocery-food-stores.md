This NaNDA dataset counts food retailers in every U.S. census tract and ZIP Code Tabulation Area (ZCTA) for each year from 1990 to 2022. It can show how access to food stores has changed in a neighborhood over three decades.

## What's Included

For grocery stores, supermarkets, meat and fish markets, fruit and vegetable markets, warehouse clubs selling food, and all food stores:

- Number of establishments
- Number of employees
- Establishments per 1,000 people
- Establishments per square mile

There are separate files for 2010 and 2020 tract boundaries and 2010 and 2020 ZCTA boundaries. Each row also has the year, total population, and land area.

## Finding Your County

In the tract files, the first five digits of the tract code (`tract_fips10` or `tract_fips20`) are the county FIPS code. ZCTA codes do not include a county, and ZCTAs can cross county lines. ZCTAs are also not the same as ZIP codes, so use a ZIP-to-ZCTA crosswalk to match ZIP code data.

## Community Use

- Find neighborhoods that have lost grocery stores or supermarkets.
- Support food access planning with long-term local trends.

## Classroom Use

- **Statistics**: Compare store density across tracts in your county.
- **Social studies**: Discuss food deserts and what store counts can and can't show.
- **Data literacy**: Compare counts, per-person rates, and per-square-mile rates for the same place.
