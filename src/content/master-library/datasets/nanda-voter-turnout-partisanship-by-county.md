This NaNDA dataset tracks voter registration and turnout in U.S. counties from 2004 to 2022. It also measures how each county has voted, using Democratic and Republican partisanship indices for 2006 to 2022.

## What's Included

- Registered voters, ballots cast, and citizen voting age population
- Registration rate, turnout among eligible voters, and turnout among registered voters
- Democratic and Republican presidential and Senate vote counts and shares
- Partisanship indices averaged over the current and previous elections

Rows are identified by county FIPS code and year. Alaska and U.S. territories are not included.

## Accessing the Data

Open the study page and log in to ICPSR to download. The file is not linked directly, so this dataset has no preview on this site.

## Why It Is Marked Sensitive

These are county totals, not records about individual voters. The file does include party vote shares and partisanship indices, so it is kept out of the student view by default. Turnout and registration can be used without the party measures.

## Community Use

- Compare your county's registration and turnout with its neighbors over time.
- See how turnout differs between presidential and midterm years.

## Classroom Use

- **Civics**: Why might turnout among eligible voters differ from turnout among registered voters?
- **Statistics**: Graph turnout trends across election years.
- **Data literacy**: Discuss how a partisanship index is built and what it leaves out.
