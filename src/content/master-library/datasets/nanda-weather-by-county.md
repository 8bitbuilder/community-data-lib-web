NaNDA's weather files give one row per day for each county in the contiguous United States and Washington, DC, from 2003 to 2016. The values come from NOAA's Global Historical Climatology Network - Daily weather stations, matched to 2010 county boundaries.

## What's Included

- Eight weather conditions, each with one file per year (112 files), including average temperature, freezing days, cold days, hot days, rainy days, and snowy days
- County FIPS code (`STCOFIPS10`) and date of observation on every row

Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories are not included, and the data stop at 2016.

## Accessing the Data

Open the study page and log in to ICPSR to download. The files are not linked directly, so this dataset has no preview on this site.

## Community Use

- See how many very hot or freezing days your county had each year.
- Line up heat or snow days with local events, school closures, or energy use.

## Classroom Use

- **Statistics**: Compare the spread of daily temperatures across seasons or counties.
- **Earth science**: Look for changes in hot and freezing days between 2003 and 2016.
- **Data literacy**: Discuss what a county value means when it comes from only a few weather stations.
