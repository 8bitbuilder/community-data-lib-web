This NaNDA dataset counts libraries in every census tract and ZIP Code Tabulation Area (ZCTA) in the United States and Puerto Rico for each year from 1992 to 2021. It is based on the Public Libraries Survey from the Institute of Museum and Library Services.

## What's Included

- Number of libraries
- Libraries per person and per square mile
- Geographic code, year, total population, and land area

There are separate files for 2010 and 2020 tract boundaries and 2010 and 2020 ZCTA boundaries.

## Finding Your County

The first five digits of an 11-digit census tract code are the county FIPS code. ZCTA codes do not include a county, and ZCTAs can cross county lines. ZCTAs are also not the same as ZIP codes, so use a ZIP-to-ZCTA crosswalk to match ZIP code data.

## Accessing the Data

Open the study page and log in to ICPSR to download. The files are not linked directly, so this dataset has no preview on this site. They come only in Stata (`.dta`) format, which Stata, R, and Python can open.

## Community Use

- See which neighborhoods have gained or lost library access since the 1990s.
- Compare library density with population in different parts of your county.

## Classroom Use

- **Statistics**: Compare library density across tracts in your county.
- **Civics**: Who is served by public libraries, and who has to travel farther to reach one?
- **Data literacy**: Why might a count of libraries change when boundaries change?
